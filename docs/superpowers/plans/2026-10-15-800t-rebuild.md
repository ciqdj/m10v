# 800T Rebuild Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development for the menu task; root implements the numerical core and integration. User approved design and explicitly waived repeated confirmation.

**Goal:** Rebuild 800T spatial effects and deliver verified GR III firmware with five stock-detail controls.
**Architecture:** Preserve JPEG capture and private journal ownership. Separate pre-color high-frequency energy scattering from color and grain; integrate native stock detail lifecycle into the unified runtime.
**Tech Stack:** Freestanding ARM C, Python/Unicorn, Node firmware containers.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-800t-rebuild-design.md

## Global Constraints
- GR III 2.10 exact official baseline only; no CFA writes or unproven GR IIIx integration.
- Five controls: gain/spread/threshold 0..8; grain/soft 0..3. Stock negative grain interaction, all five carrier slots, only 800T.
- No overwrite of uploaded files. Preserve selected existing extensions and strict artifact audits.
- Host/ARM checks are not real camera validation.

## Review Focus
- Non-800T detail list unchanged and private IDs never enter stock tone.
- SD failure, cancel, reset, switching slots and restarting preserve intended values.
- Sparse highlights shift within coarse cell without disappearing; broad lights are not shape-classified away.
- Grain and soft diffusion never generate halation seeds.
- Uploaded old aggressive state cannot silently reassert obsolete profile behavior.
