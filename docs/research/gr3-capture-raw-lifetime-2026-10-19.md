RAW allocation starts by invalidating the token, even on failure. Nonzero RAW setter invalidates before the original setter, including transfer-in. A zero setter outside the active successful release sequence invalidates, including transfer-out. This prevents a previously released transaction from authorizing a subsequent frame or a different owner. Original allocation, free, setter and transfer behavior is unchanged.

The exported capture API is `m10v_save_trace_recipe_raw_status(recipe,shooting,backing,source)`:0 reject,1 live,2 attested released.
